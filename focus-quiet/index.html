Step 1

Interruptions Down

DND, batch notifications, device‑free rooms. Replace micro‑checks with one intentional check block.

  • 1 hour device‑free daily
  • App limits; remove 1–2 triggers weekly
  • Grayscale after 9pm
Step 2

Single Task, Deep Work

Set a narrow target, clear desk, choose one task. Small win → momentum.

  • Define end state in one line
  • 10–20 min starter sprint
  • Headphones = office door
Step 3

Sustain with Rests

Pulse work/rest. End sessions cleanly: save, jot next step, close tabs.

  • 50/10 cadence or 25/5
  • Eyes‑far and posture reset
  • Movement snack
Why it matters

Benefits

  • Restored attention: fewer interruptions → deeper work
  • Lower stress: less notification load → calmer baseline
  • Real connection: more in‑person time
  • Healthier reward loops: break compulsive scrolling
  • Physical relief: eyes, neck, and sleep benefit
Watchouts

Risks of Excessive Screen Time

  • Compulsive loops and doomscrolling
  • Over‑sexualized feeds distort body image
  • Comparison/FOMO → mood hits
  • Sedentary strain and sleep disruption
Escape

Escaping Algorithms & Marketing

  • Limit tracking (privacy tools, ad blockers)
  • Research purchases outside ad funnels
  • Set clear online goals → avoid aimless scroll
Infra

Real‑Life Alternatives

  • Nature immersion
  • Creative pursuits
  • Face‑to‑face time
  • Mindful practices
  • Movement
Perspective

The Enduring Power of Philosophy

Use simple claims, act with integrity, accept limits, cultivate wonder. Philosophy gives language to choose what matters when the feed tugs elsewhere.
